Healthcare Professional
Consulting

Consultation for mental health professionals and physicians on patient communication, treatment challenges, and professional development.

Expert Guidance for Professionals

A Trusted Resource for Colleagues

With decades of clinical and academic experience, Dr. Gardner offers consultation to mental health therapists, psychiatrists, and physicians who are navigating complex patient situations, looking to strengthen their clinical approach, or seeking guidance on professional challenges.

Drawing on his background in psychiatry, psychoanalysis, and traumatic brain injury, Dr. Gardner provides thoughtful, nuanced consultation that goes beyond surface-level advice — helping colleagues think more deeply about their patients and their practice.

All consultations are conducted via secure video conference or phone, offering flexible, accessible support for healthcare professionals throughout the San Diego region and beyond.

Areas of Consultation

How Dr. Gardner Can Help

Clinical Case Consultation

Thoughtful discussion of complex or challenging patient cases, including treatment planning, diagnostic considerations, and therapeutic approach.

  • Difficult-to-treat patients and stuck therapeutic relationships

  • Psychiatric and psychodynamic formulation

  • TBI-related behavioral and emotional presentations

  • Medication-behavior intersection and psychopharmacology guidance

Patient Communication

Practical guidance on improving doctor-patient and therapist-patient communication — building stronger therapeutic alliances and achieving better clinical outcomes.

  • Engaging difficult or resistant patients

  • Delivering difficult news and sensitive diagnoses

  • Improving treatment adherence and shared decision-making

  • Managing countertransference and professional boundaries

Professional Development

Guidance for healthcare professionals seeking to grow their practice, navigate career transitions, or strengthen their leadership and clinical skills.

  • Practice development and growth strategies

  • Career transitions and professional identity

  • Preventing and recovering from professional burnout

  • Leadership effectiveness and team communication

Why Dr. Gardner

Depth of Experience

Dr. Gardner brings a rare combination of board-certified psychiatry, psychoanalytic training, and extensive experience in traumatic brain injury — making him uniquely equipped to advise colleagues across a wide range of complex clinical and professional situations.

He has served as a voluntary associate clinical professor at UCSD, Medical Director of brain injury rehabilitation programs, and has been recognized repeatedly as one of San Diego's Physicians of Exceptional Excellence.

Selected Credentials

  • Diplomate, American Board of Psychiatry & Neurology

  • Distinguished Life Fellow, American Psychiatric Association

  • Graduate, Adult Psychoanalysis — San Diego Psychoanalytic Society and Institute

  • Former Voluntary Associate Clinical Professor, UCSD School of Medicine

  • Medical Director, Brain Injury Rehabilitation Programs

  • Top Doctor, San Diego Physicians of Exceptional Excellence (multiple years)
