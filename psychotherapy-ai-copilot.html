Coria Training

Your AI-Powered Educational Copilot

Created by Dan Gardner, MD and produced by ExpertFocus LLC, Coria Training is a specialized AI platform designed to help mental health professionals and trainees build clinical confidence through realistic, private practice. Available 24/7, it bridges the gap between theoretical knowledge and real-world therapeutic skill.

This isn't a replacement for clinical supervision — it's a powerful supplement. Coria Training gives you a private space to experiment, refine your approach, and deepen your clinical instincts between sessions and supervision meetings.

Launch Coria Training

What It Does

Core Features

Simulated Client Interactions

Practice therapeutic interventions with realistic AI-generated client responses based on specific clinical presentations. Test new approaches before a live session with immediate, model-adherent feedback.

Complete Confidentiality

Practice in a fully private environment with no patient data involved. Explore new techniques, work through uncertainty, and build clinical confidence without any professional risk.

Personalized, Data-Driven Training

Receive tailored feedback aligned with therapeutic models. The platform adapts to your learning needs, helping you identify patterns, strengthen areas of uncertainty, and track professional growth over time.

Available 24/7

Practice on your schedule — before a session, after supervision, or late at night. Coria Training is available whenever inspiration strikes or a clinical challenge arises.

Bridge Theory & Practice

Move beyond textbook knowledge into applied clinical skill. Coria Training helps you translate what you learn in coursework and supervision into fluent, confident therapeutic action.

Build Confidence

Sharpen clinical instincts and enter every session with greater certainty. Repeated, safe practice accelerates the natural development of therapeutic expertise and professional identity.

Who It's For

Designed for Mental Health Professionals

Trainees & Graduate Students

Accelerate skill acquisition during clinical training programs.

Early-Career Clinicians

Build confidence in newly acquired techniques before applying them with clients.

Experienced Therapists

Explore new modalities or refresh skills in a risk-free environment.

Supervisors & Educators

Recommend as a structured between-supervision learning resource for trainees.
