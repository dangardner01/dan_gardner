Patient Communities

Online health support communities for mental health, brain injury, cancer, diabetes, and other chronic conditions — connecting patients, families, and caregivers.

Online Communities

Health Support Communities

Connect with others who understand what you're going through — curated by Dan Gardner, MD.

American Cancer Society — Cancer Survivors Network

A community for cancer patients, survivors, and caregivers to connect and share experiences.

csn.cancer.org

American Diabetes Association Community

Connect with others managing diabetes — share tips, ask questions, and find support.

community.diabetes.org

Association of Cancer Online Resources

A large collection of online support communities focused on cancer and related conditions.

acor.org

CareFlash

Free service that facilitates communication between friends and loved ones during a healthcare event.

careflash.com

DailyStrength

Social network of support communities serving hundreds of thousands of registered users across many conditions.

dailystrength.org

Everyday Health

A place to join discussions, share stories, and find people who understand your health journey.

everydayhealth.com

Inspire

A patient collaboration site connecting people with similar health conditions to share and support one another.

inspire.com

Lotsa Helping Hands

Create support communities and coordinate volunteers to help a friend or family member in need.

lotsahelpinghands.com

MDJunction

A health-related social network with support groups for hundreds of medical conditions.

mdjunction.com

MedHelp

Patient and expert-generated content on specific conditions, with tools to track health and connect with others.

medhelp.org

Patients Like Me

Tools for patients to track their treatments, status, and outcomes — and connect with similar patients.

patientslikeme.com

PsychCentral

Mental health social network and forums — peer support for depression, anxiety, bipolar disorder, and more.

forums.psychcentral.com

Sophia's Garden

A healing community for parents and children facing serious illness, offering compassionate support and resources.

sophiasgarden.org

TuDiabetes

An active community for people touched by diabetes — sharing experiences, tips, and mutual support.

tudiabetes.org
