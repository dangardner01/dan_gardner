About

A Career Defined by Discovery & Leadership

Robert Conn, PhD is a distinguished engineer and academic statesman whose career spans decades of transformative work at the intersection of frontier science, higher education, and public policy. As a Professor of Engineering and longtime faculty member at the University of California, San Diego, he built a reputation for combining intellectual rigor with institutional vision — culminating in his tenure as Dean of the Irwin and Joan Jacobs School of Engineering at UCSD, where he led the school through a period of sustained growth, curricular innovation, and elevated research output.

Dr. Conn subsequently brought his leadership capabilities to bear at a national scale as Director of the Kavli Foundation, one of the world's most influential philanthropies dedicated to advancing basic science research. In that role, he steered major investments across cosmology, nanoscience, neuroscience, and theoretical physics — shaping the trajectories of research institutions and scientists worldwide. His tenure exemplified the rare capacity to translate scientific depth into strategic philanthropy.

Today, Dr. Conn applies four decades of expertise as an independent consultant, advising universities, national laboratories, government agencies, and research-driven foundations on strategic planning, research leadership, science policy, and the cultivation of high-impact scientific programs. His counsel is grounded in lived experience and distinguished by a commitment to advancing science as a public good.

"The measure of a scientific career is not only in the discoveries made, but in the institutions strengthened and the scientists inspired along the way."
