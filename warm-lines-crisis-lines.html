Warm Lines & Crisis Lines

Free emotional support lines, online chat resources, and 24/7 crisis and suicide prevention hotlines — for when you need someone to talk to.

Online Resources

Free Online Chat Support

Anonymous, free, and available from any device.

IMAlive

A live online network using instant messaging to respond to people in crisis. The first online network with 100% of volunteers trained and certified in crisis intervention.

imalive.org/online

7 Cups

Connects you to caring listeners for free emotional support. 180 professional therapists and over 300,000 trained listeners have helped more than 25 million people.

7cups.com

Non-Crisis Support

Warm Lines

Warm lines are non-emergency emotional support lines — for when you need someone to talk to but are not in immediate crisis.

San Diego Warm Lines

  • NAMI San Diego
  • Post-Partum Health Alliance
  • Alzheimer's Association
  • Meeting Place Clubhouse, Inc.

OC Warmline

NAMI Orange County warmline for non-emergency emotional support.

ochealthinfo.com

LA Warmline

Project Return Peer Support Network — Los Angeles peer-run warmline.

prpsn.org

SF Peer-Run Warmline

San Francisco peer-run warmline offering compassionate, non-judgmental support.

mentalhealthsf.org

24 / 7 Help

Crisis & Suicide Prevention Lines

Free, confidential, and available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Call or text 988 — the national suicide and crisis lifeline, available 24/7 in English and Spanish. Also available online at 988lifeline.org.

San Diego

UP2SD — suicide prevention and crisis resources for San Diego County.

up2sd.org

Orange County

OC Health Care Agency — 24/7 mental health crisis hotline for Orange County.

ochealthinfo.com

Los Angeles

LA County Public Health — suicide hotlines and crisis resources for Los Angeles.

publichealth.lacounty.gov

San Francisco

San Francisco Suicide Prevention — crisis intervention and suicide prevention resources.

sfsuicide.org

California Statewide

Comprehensive directory of suicide hotlines across California.

suicidehotlines.com
