How to Use This Page

Quick-Access TBI Medical Literature

Each link below opens a pre-filtered search of peer-reviewed TBI medical literature. Results are drawn from PubMed (the U.S. National Library of Medicine's primary biomedical database) and MedlinePlus (consumer-friendly health information).

Review = PubMed systematic/narrative reviews Consumer = MedlinePlus plain-language info

Tip: You can sort PubMed results by publication date or relevance using the "Sort by" menu at the top of the results page.
