TBI Recovery Videos

Curated YouTube playlists covering every stage of traumatic brain injury recovery — from understanding your injury to rebuilding independence with assistive technology.

Video Resources

Learn at Your Own Pace

These curated video playlists were assembled to help TBI survivors, their families, and healthcare providers understand the full landscape of traumatic brain injury — from its immediate effects to the long road of recovery, rehabilitation, and adaptation.

Each playlist focuses on a distinct aspect of TBI: foundational education, practical recovery strategies, real survivor stories, assistive and peripheral technology, and the science of neuropsychology. Together they offer a comprehensive, accessible resource that complements professional care.
