About the Podcast

Voices from the TBI Community

The TBI Recovery Podcast brings together the voices of people who know brain injury from the inside — survivors navigating life after TBI, families supporting loved ones through recovery, and the healthcare professionals and rehabilitation specialists working alongside them.

Each episode offers an honest, human perspective on what recovery truly looks like: the setbacks, the breakthroughs, and the strategies that make a real difference. These conversations are designed to inform, inspire, and connect the broader TBI community.
