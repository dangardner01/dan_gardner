Digital Tools

A set of AI and software tools Dr. Gardner has independently built and continues to develop — spanning clinical practice support, personal reflection, and general-purpose utilities. Each is labeled plainly with its current stage, from live products to early research prototypes.

Available — live and open to use Pilot / limited release — working, in testing Research prototype — experimental Archived project — no longer maintained

For Clinicians

Clinical & Professional Practice

Tools for training, self-reflection, case consultation, and practice operations. These are educational and workflow tools — not medical devices. They do not diagnose, treat, or replace clinical judgment, supervision, or professional consultation.

Available

FlowGap

Practice Ops · Self-Assessment

A self-assessment for mental health practices: ten short modules score scheduling, billing, retention, and compliance against industry benchmarks, then flag where time and revenue are likely leaking.

Visit
Research prototype

Coria Training

Clinical Ed · Training

An AI training copilot for psychotherapy graduate students — case-based practice and psychodynamically-informed feedback on clinical reasoning, grounded in established clinical frameworks.
Pilot / limited release

Psychotherapy Skills Assessment

Self-Reflection · Practice

A self-guided practice exercise: work through realistic clinical scenarios and get AI feedback on empathy, crisis response, and professional boundaries. A reflection tool, not a validated clinical assessment.
Research prototype

The Consulting Room

Psychoanalysis · Clinical AI

Text-based sessions with AI-simulated versions of 45 psychoanalysts across 12 schools of thought — Freudian, Kleinian, Jungian, Lacanian, and more. Each persona draws on published theory and clinical language.
Pilot / limited release

Reverim

Local AI · Clinical Search

A local, offline AI search tool for your own clinical notes and documents — nothing leaves your machine. Every answer is checked against your own material before it's shown to you.

Visit

For Personal Use

Reflection & Wellbeing

Tools for personal reflection, caregiving support, and everyday emotional regulation. These are not therapy, counseling, or crisis services. If you're in crisis, contact 988 (US) or your local emergency services.

Available

Dialoque - Difficult Conversations

Voice AI · Communication

AI voice roleplay for rehearsing the workplace conversations you've been avoiding. Unscripted AI personas respond in character, and structured scorecards show where your communication landed.

Visit
Pilot / limited release

Undertone Journal

Reflection · Journaling

A reflective journal that reads your entries through a psychodynamic lens — surfacing the pattern underneath a feeling (guilt, ambivalence, envy) and matching it to a curated quote.

Visit
Archived project

CareCompanion Coach

Voice AI · Caregiving

AI voice support for family caregivers — guidance, a place to talk through the day, and practical resources, available around the clock for people navigating a caregiving role largely alone.
Pilot / limited release

Attune

Adaptive Audio · Infant Soothing

Adaptive music for settling a baby: tell it how your little one feels right now — awake, fussy, sleepy, content — and the music shifts, piece by piece, toward calm.

Visit
Pilot / limited release

Introvue

AI Interview · Self-Reflection

An AI-guided self-reflection tool that interviews you like a sharp, curious friend to surface a blind spot — then produces an evidence-backed hypothesis about what's really going on, plus a small commitment to test it against real life.

Visit

General Purpose

Everyday Tools

General-purpose tools that came out of the same practice of building focused, well-scoped software — not clinical in nature.

Available

Story Distiller

Reading · Distillation

Citation Verification
